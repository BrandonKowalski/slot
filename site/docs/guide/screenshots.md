# Screenshots

## Taking one

You can take a screenshot by pressing <kbd>SELECT</kbd> + <kbd>X</kbd> while you play.

## The gallery

Tap <kbd>MENU</kbd> in game and pick Screenshots. It shows the screenshots for the game you're playing, three across,
newest first.

The row only shows up once you've taken one.

| Button            | Does                            |
|-------------------|---------------------------------|
| D-pad             | Move around the gallery         |
| <kbd>A</kbd>      | View the screenshot full screen |
| <kbd>Y</kbd>      | Delete it                       |
| <kbd>SELECT</kbd> | Select several                  |
| <kbd>B</kbd>      | Back                            |

### Full screen

<kbd>Left</kbd> and <kbd>Right</kbd> step through them. 

<kbd>A</kbd> zooms in, up to 4x, and the D-pad pans around. <kbd>B</kbd> zooms back out, then goes back to the gallery.

### Deleting several

Press <kbd>SELECT</kbd> in the gallery, tick the ones you want with <kbd>A</kbd>, then press <kbd>Y</kbd> to delete
them. <kbd>B</kbd> or <kbd>SELECT</kbd> leaves without deleting.

Once a screenshot is gone, it's gone.

## On the card

Screenshots are saved as `.png` files in `Screenshots/<system>/<game>/`.
