# Lid, Sleep and Power

## Closing the lid

Close the lid and the screen turns off and the game pauses. slot saves your place and your battery save on the way down. Open it again and you're back in the game.

Leave it shut and slot powers off after 3 minutes. Next time you power on, it boots straight back into that game.

Change the wait with Lid Power Off in Settings → General: 1, 3, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes.

## No lid?

On devices without a lid, tap <kbd>POWER</kbd> to sleep and tap it again to wake. The Lid Power Off timer works the same way.

## Powering off

Hold <kbd>POWER</kbd> for a second. slot saves, then powers off.

When the battery hits 5% and you're not charging, slot saves and powers off by itself so a dead battery can't eat your progress.

## Restarting

Settings → About, then <kbd>X</kbd>. Handy after you've added games or labels, since slot only looks for those when it boots.

## The clock

The first time slot boots, or if the clock ever reads as long ago, it asks you to set the time. You can change it later from Date & Time in Settings → General.

<kbd>Left</kbd> and <kbd>Right</kbd> pick a field, <kbd>Up</kbd> and <kbd>Down</kbd> change it, <kbd>A</kbd> sets it. The clock is 24-hour, and the last field is your time zone as an offset from UTC.

Games with a clock on the cart, like Pokémon Gold, Silver, Crystal, Ruby and Sapphire, use it.
