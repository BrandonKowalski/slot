# Speed

## Fast Forward

Hold <kbd>R2</kbd> to fast-forward. Let go and you're back to normal.

Double tap <kbd>R2</kbd> to lock it on. Press it again to unlock.

Settings → Gameplay → Fast Forward sets the cap: 2×, 3×, 4× or 6×. Some games are heavier than others, and slot runs as fast as it can up to the cap. mGBA uses more system resources than gpSP and will have a slower top fast forward speed.

You can optionally turn on Fast Forward Sound. slot mutes fast forward by default because it sounds like chipmunks and drives me nuts.

## Rewind

Hold <kbd>L2</kbd> to go back in time.

How far back you can go depends on the game, since some games have larger state files for restore.

Rewind only records at normal speed. Turn it off in Settings → Gameplay if you don't want it.

## Turbo

By default, hold <kbd>X</kbd> for turbo <kbd>A</kbd> and <kbd>Y</kbd> for turbo <kbd>B</kbd>. They fire about 10 times a second.

Turbo Buttons in Settings → Gameplay turns them off. You can change them to other buttons in [Controls](controls.md).

## While linked

Fast-forward, rewind and loading states are all disabled while you're linked.
