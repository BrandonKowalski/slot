# Speed

## Fast-forward

Hold <kbd>R2</kbd> to fast-forward. Let go and you're back to normal.

Double tap <kbd>R2</kbd> to lock it on. Press it again to unlock.

Settings → Gameplay → Fast Forward sets how fast: 2×, 3×, 4× or 6×. That's a cap, not a promise. Some games are heavier than others, and slot runs as fast as it can up to the cap. Set it to Off and holding the button does nothing, which is handy if you keep hitting it by accident.

Fast Forward Sound turns the sound on while fast-forwarding. It's off by default because it sounds like chipmunks.

## Rewind

Hold <kbd>L2</kbd> to go back in time.

slot keeps the last stretch of play in memory. How far back you can go depends on the game, since some games are bigger to remember than others.

Rewind only records at normal speed, so fast-forwarded bits can't be rewound. Turn it off in Settings → Gameplay if you don't want it.

## Turbo

Hold <kbd>X</kbd> for turbo <kbd>A</kbd> and <kbd>Y</kbd> for turbo <kbd>B</kbd>. They fire about 10 times a second.

Turbo Buttons in Settings → Gameplay turns them off. You can move them to other buttons in [Controls](controls.md).

## While linked

Fast-forward, rewind and loading states are all off while you're linked with someone. Your friend probably wouldn't appreciate it.
